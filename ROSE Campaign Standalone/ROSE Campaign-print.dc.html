Campaign Overview

This campaign was created for ROSE, a flower brand that turns simple moments into meaningful memories through carefully designed floral arrangements.

The campaign focuses on presenting flowers as a language of emotions — expressing love, appreciation, celebration, gratitude, and thoughtful gestures.
